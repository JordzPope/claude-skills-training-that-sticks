# Hallowmere Facilities (fictional)

Hallowmere Facilities is a 1,400-person facilities services firm in the North of England. It
maintains buildings for hospitals, schools, councils and offices on multi-year contracts: planned
maintenance, reactive repairs, cleaning and security. Head office and the customer contact centre
are in Leeds; engineers work from depots in Newcastle, Manchester, Sheffield and Hull.

Every skill in this pack uses Hallowmere for its sample input, so the pack reads as one place.
The programmes below cover very different content on purpose: the skills work the same way on a
management programme, a systems rollout, a customer course and mandatory safety training.

## Structure

| Area | People | Who's in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field operations | about 880 | 610 engineers (electrical, mechanical, multi-skilled), 46 engineering supervisors, 4 regional operations managers, planners |
| Customer contact centre, Leeds | about 220 | 180 customer advisers, 18 team leaders, 2 contact centre managers |
| Cleaning and security | about 210 | site supervisors and operatives on client sites |
| Head office | about 90 | finance, contracts, health and safety, IT, people team |

The people team includes a Head of L&D (the person using this pack), an L&D business partner, a
learning designer, two trainers and an L&D coordinator. The Head of L&D reports to the Director of
People. Learning runs through an LMS; staff message each other on Microsoft Teams. Engineers work
from a phone and a van, and rarely sit at a desk.

## The numbers leaders watch (fictional)

| Measure | Where it stands |
| --- | --- |
| First-time fix rate (reactive jobs) | 71% against a contract target of 80% |
| Complaints reopened within 28 days | 1 in 5 |
| Contact centre adviser turnover | 38% a year |
| Contract SLA breaches | about 40 a month across all contracts |
| Near misses reported | rising since a new reporting app went live |

## Programmes running this year

| Programme | Audience | Content type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead the Shift** | 46 engineering supervisors and 18 contact centre team leaders, most promoted from the tools or the phones | first-line management: one-to-ones, feedback, absence conversations |
| **FieldPro rollout** | 610 engineers and the planners | a new job-management app on engineers' phones: job notes, parts, photos, sign-off |
| **Complaints That Close** | 180 customer advisers | handling a complaint so it stays closed: listening, ownership, the right fix first time |
| **Asbestos Awareness refresher** | all engineers and site supervisors, every year | mandatory safety training under the organisation's health and safety procedures |
| **Contract Conversations** | 12 contract managers | difficult client conversations about scope, cost and SLA breaches |
