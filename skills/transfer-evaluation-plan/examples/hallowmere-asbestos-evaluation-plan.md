# Asbestos Awareness refresher 2027: evaluation plan (draft v1)

Prepared by: L&D coordinator, for the Head of L&D and the health and safety manager
Date: 20 October 2026

## The programme

The Asbestos Awareness refresher is mandatory every year for all 610 engineers and every site supervisor. It is a 90-minute e-learning module on the LMS, followed by a 20-minute toolbox talk at each depot led by an engineering supervisor. The 2027 refresher opens on 4 January and must be completed by 31 March 2027.

What we want engineers to do on the job:

- Check the client's asbestos register before starting any job that disturbs the fabric of a building (drilling, cutting, lifting floor tiles, opening ceiling voids).
- Stop work and tell their supervisor if they find material they suspect contains asbestos and it isn't on the register.

## Data we already hold

- LMS: completions, quiz scores, and the end-of-module feedback form (an overall rating out of 5, plus "How relevant was this to your job?" out of 5).
- FieldPro: since September 2026, every reactive job that involves drilling or cutting asks the engineer to tick "asbestos register checked" before the job notes open.
- The health and safety manager's monthly safety report: incidents, near misses and stop-work reports.
- Engineering supervisors' site visit checklist, used on four visits per engineer a year.
- Contracts team: SLA breaches by contract, each month.

| 4 Results | Contract SLA breaches | Down from about 40 a month to 30 a month by June 2027 | Contracts team | Contract managers | Quarterly |

## Reporting

- Monthly operations report, January to March 2027: completion by depot, and quiz scores by engineer so the regional operations managers can see who is falling behind.
- Year-end report to the Director of Operations and the board's health and safety committee: all six measures against target.
