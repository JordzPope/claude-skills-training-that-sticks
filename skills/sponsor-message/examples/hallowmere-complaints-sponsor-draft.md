# Sponsor message for Complaints That Close (first draft from the Head of Customer Operations)

## From the programme plan, agreed with the Head of L&D

- Programme: Complaints That Close, half-day workshops for all 180 customer advisers at the Leeds contact centre, in groups of 12, March to May 2027.
- Business measure: complaints reopened within 28 days. Now: 1 in 5. Target by December 2027: 1 in 10.
- Behaviours: play the complaint back to the customer and agree it before offering a fix; give the customer a named next step and a date before ending the call.
- Each adviser's team leader holds a 15-minute conversation with them before their workshop.
- Agreed by the Head of Customer Operations: each adviser's call-handling-time target is paused for the two weeks after their workshop.

## Draft message

Subject: Complaints That Close: a world-class programme for a world-class team

Hi everyone,

I'm delighted to announce Complaints That Close, the most exciting programme this contact centre has ever run. It will revolutionise the way we handle every complaint, and I know it will make us the best customer team in the industry.

Right now nearly half of the complaints we close are reopened within 28 days. That's not good enough for our customers or for us.

In your workshop you'll practise playing the complaint back to the customer before offering a fix, and giving them a named next step and a date before you end the call. Your team leader will talk to you about it beforehand.

To give you room to put it into practice, your call-handling-time target is paused for two weeks after your workshop.

From June we'll share a monthly league table of every adviser's reopened complaints, so we can celebrate the most improved. And to be clear, anyone whose reopen rate hasn't come down by the summer will be picked up in their performance review.

See you in March,
Head of Customer Operations
