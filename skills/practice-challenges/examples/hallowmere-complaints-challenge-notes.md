# Complaints That Close: ideas for post-course challenges (trainer's rough notes)

From: trainer, contact centre · To: learning designer · Friday 23 October 2026

Hi, here's my brain dump for the challenges after cohort 4. Can you turn it into something we can send out?

**Who:** cohort 4 is 24 advisers from the general repairs queue, all on the early and late rota. They handle stage 1 complaints on the phone: log it, own it, fix it or book the fix. Anything that goes to stage 2 gets passed to the complaints resolution team at head office, who take it from there. AHT target on the repairs queue is 6 minutes and the team leaders review it every day on the wallboard.

**Course:** two half-days, Tuesday 10 and Thursday 12 November.

**The three bits I really want to stick:**

- Listen first: let the customer finish, then play the problem back in their words before offering anything.
- Own it: give your name and say exactly what you'll do and by when.
- Right fix first time: check the job history before booking another visit, because a lot of our reopened complaints are a second engineer turning up to the same fault with no notes.

**Challenge ideas, one a week for the first month:**

- Week 1, "Listen longer": on every complaint call this week, spend an extra five minutes letting the customer talk and playing it back before you offer a fix. Tell them not to worry about AHT for the week.
- Week 2, "Own it": give your name and a specific callback time on every complaint, then make the callback when you said you would. Keep a tally of how many you hit.
- Week 3, "See it through": take one stage 2 complaint and follow it all the way to resolution, including the final letter to the customer.
- Week 4, "Look back": write a 500-word reflection on what you've learned over the four weeks and email it to me by the Friday.

**Making it fun:** a leaderboard on the team wallboard next to the AHT figures, one point per challenge done and a bonus point for every customer compliment, the top three names read out at the Friday huddle, and a voucher for the top adviser each week. Team leaders can tick off who's done what.

Thanks!
