# Learning impact map: Complaints That Close (draft)

**Programme:** Complaints That Close, a one-day course in Leeds, then two 45-minute team huddles in weeks 2 and 6
**For:** 180 customer advisers, Leeds contact centre
**Sponsor:** Head of Customer Operations
**Prepared by:** learning designer, 16 October 2026

## Course outline

| Time | Session |
| --- | --- |
| 09.30 | The history and structure of Hallowmere's complaints team |
| 10.30 | Listening and summarising back: practice in pairs on recorded calls |
| 12.00 | The complaints policy and escalation routes |
| 13.30 | Owning a complaint to the end: confirming the fix with the customer (role play) |
| 15.30 | Your first three complaints back on the phones (action plan) |

## Notes from the sponsor (call, 9 October 2026)

- 1 in 5 complaints reopen within 28 days.
- In a sample of 50 complaints reopened in August, 34 were repeat faults: the engineer visited, the fault came back and the customer rang again.
- Advisers often close a complaint as soon as the engineer's visit is booked, before anyone checks the fix with the customer.
- Field operations are working on first-time fix separately. It's at 71% against a contract target of 80%.

## Business goals (2026-27 business plan)

- G1 Keep all three NHS trust contracts at renewal in 2027.
- G2 Reduce contract SLA breaches, now about 40 a month across all contracts.
