## Intro (shown before question 1)

You did [programme] in [month]. This survey asks what you've used at work since, and what made it easier or harder. It takes about [five] minutes. It's [anonymous: we can't see who answered]. We only report results for groups of [10] or more people, and we'll share what we found and what we're changing by [date]. If you'd like the survey in another format, or help to fill it in, contact [role or mailbox].

## Questions

**Use** (one question per behaviour, two to six behaviours)

1. In the last four weeks, when [situation] came up, how often did you [behaviour 1]?
   - It hasn't come up
   - It came up and I didn't try it
   - I tried it once or twice
   - I did it most times it came up
2. [Repeat for behaviour 2, with the same four options.]

**What got in the way**

3. What, if anything, has made it harder to do these things at work? Tick all that apply.
   - I'm not sure how to do it yet
   - The course didn't match my job
   - I've forgotten parts of it
   - No time
   - Targets or KPIs pull the other way
   - Tools or systems don't support it
   - My manager hasn't talked to me about it
   - No chance to use it yet
   - Nothing has got in the way
   - Something else (please say): [open box]

**Support**

4. Did your manager talk with you about [programme]?
   - Before the course
   - After the course
   - Both
   - Neither
5. What has helped you use it, if anything? Tick all that apply.
   - [the programme's follow-up supports: manager check-ins, job aid, reminders, peer group, practice challenges]
   - Nothing yet
   - Something else (please say): [open box]

**What changed**

6. Has anything changed in your work since you started doing this?
   - Nothing yet
   - A small change I could describe
   - A clear change other people have noticed
7. Describe one time in the last month you did [behaviour 1 or any behaviour]. What happened? [open box]

**What next**

8. What would help you do this more? [open box]

[Optional, only if every group has 10 or more people: 9. Which of these best describes your area? [broad groups only].]
