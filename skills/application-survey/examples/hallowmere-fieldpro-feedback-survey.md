# FieldPro training: feedback survey (draft v1)

Prepared by: L&D coordinator, for the Head of L&D to approve
Date: 26 October 2026

## About the training

FieldPro is the new job-management app on engineers' phones: job notes, parts, photos and sign-off. All 610 engineers and the planners attend a half-day session at their depot (Newcastle, Manchester, Sheffield or Hull) between 9 November and 18 December 2026. Engineers switch to FieldPro for every job from 4 January 2027.

What we want people doing on the job:

- Writing job notes in FieldPro before leaving site, under the fault, cause and fix headings.
- Attaching before and after photos to every reactive job.
- Ordering parts through FieldPro from the job, so planners aren't taking phone calls for them.
- Getting the client's sign-off in the app before closing the job.

## How it goes out

A QR code on the last slide of every session. Engineers fill it in on their phones before they leave the room, and the trainer reminds anyone who hasn't.

## Intro text

Thanks for coming to FieldPro training today! It takes 3 minutes. Tell us what you thought so we can keep improving.

## Questions

1. Your employee number (so we can match your answers to your FieldPro usage once you're live)
2. Your depot: Newcastle / Manchester / Sheffield / Hull
3. Overall, how satisfied were you with today's session? (1 to 5 stars)
4. How would you rate the trainer? (1 to 5 stars)
5. Was the room at your depot comfortable and well set up? (Yes / No)
6. I feel confident using FieldPro on my next job. (Strongly agree / Agree / Neutral / Disagree / Strongly disagree)
7. How much will FieldPro improve the way you record job notes? (A little / Quite a lot / A great deal)
8. How likely are you to recommend this session to a colleague? (0 to 10)
9. Anything else you'd like to tell us?
