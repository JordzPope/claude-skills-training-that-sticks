# Transfer risk audit: Asbestos Awareness refresher 2026-27 (draft)

Prepared by the L&D coordinator for the Head of L&D, 21 October 2026.

## The programme

- **Audience:** all 610 engineers, plus the site supervisors on client sites. Required every year.
- **Format:** a 60-minute e-learning module on the LMS, then a 20-question quiz with a pass mark of 80%. Engineers can do it on their phone or on a depot PC.
- **When:** any time in November or December 2026. The LMS sends reminders at two and four weeks.
- **Content:** what asbestos is and where it turns up in buildings built before 2000; the health risks; checking the client's asbestos register before starting work; what to do if you suspect asbestos (stop, keep others away, report it to your supervisor and the health and safety manager).
- **Content owner:** the health and safety manager reviews and approves the content each year.

## The work it applies to

- Before any job that disturbs the building fabric (drilling, lifting floor tiles, opening a ceiling void) in a building built before 2000, the engineer must check the client's asbestos register for that area.
- The registers are PDFs on the head office SharePoint site, one per client site. FieldPro doesn't link to them, and most engineers' phones can't open SharePoint.
- The planners book reactive jobs back to back, and FieldPro job times don't include time for a register check.
- 14 asbestos-related near misses were reported in 2025-26. In 9 of them the engineer had started drilling or lifting tiles without checking the register.

## Audit

### Learner factors

**Risk: high.** The older engineers, mainly at Hull and Sheffield, have sat this course every year for 15 years or more and switch off. Many click straight through to the quiz.

**Fix:** raise the pass mark to 90% and send each depot's engineering supervisors a list of the 10% lowest-scoring engineers by name.

### Training design

**Risk: medium.** The e-learning has clear photos of where asbestos is found. There's no practice at finding an area on a real register.

**Fix:** add two scenarios where engineers look up a job's area on a sample register. Owner: learning designer, by 15 November 2026.

### Transfer matrix

| | Before | During | After |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Manager (engineering supervisor) | Not needed: supervisors are stretched and it's an e-learning. Risk: low | Not needed | Not needed |
| Trainer or L&D | LMS enrolment and reminders | E-learning and quiz | Completion report to the Director of Operations |
| Learner | Find an hour to do it | Complete the e-learning and pass the quiz | Apply it on site |

### Evidence that it transfers

Last year 100% of engineers completed by 31 December, with an average quiz score of 92%, which shows engineers are applying what they learned. The LMS marks each engineer competent when they pass, and the trainer signs the record.

## Summary

Overall risk: low. The main risk is engagement among the older engineers, which the higher pass mark will deal with.
