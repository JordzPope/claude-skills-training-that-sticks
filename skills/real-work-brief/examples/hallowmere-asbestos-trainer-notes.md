# Notes for the real work brief: Asbestos Awareness refresher

From: trainer, health and safety courses
For: the L&D coordinator, to turn into the brief that goes out to everyone booked

**Course:** Asbestos Awareness refresher, half a day. Hull depot on 9 March 2027 and Newcastle depot on 11 March 2027. Up to 24 people in each group: engineers, plus the cleaning and security site supervisors for those areas.

**What I'll do with the tasks:** in the second hour, people work in pairs on their own job. They look up the building in the asbestos register, spot where the job could disturb asbestos-containing materials, and agree the point where they'd stop work and report it.

**Notes for the brief:**

- Everyone does reactive jobs, so everyone will have one. Ask them to pick a job from the last two months where they drilled, cut or lifted something in a building put up before 2000.
- To make it real, they should photograph the asbestos register page and the site contact sheet for that building and send both to me on WhatsApp.
- They can fill it in at the depot at the start of a shift.
- Brief goes out on 22 February. Photos back to me by 15 March so I've got time to sort everyone into pairs.
- At the end I'll go through each person's job. If they've spotted the right stopping point, I'll sign them off as asbestos competent for the year on their training record.
