# Lead the Shift, cohort 3: manager briefing (draft 2)

From: L&D business partner
To: the four regional operations managers
Programme: Lead the Shift, cohort 3. Twelve engineering supervisors, two days, Tuesday 9 and Wednesday 10 February 2027, Sheffield depot training room.
Covers: one-to-ones, giving feedback, absence conversations.
Send date: Monday 18 January 2027

## Email

Subject: Lead the Shift, 9 and 10 February: a 15-minute conversation before it starts

Hi all,

Twelve of your engineering supervisors start Lead the Shift on 9 and 10 February at the Sheffield depot. Most of them came up from the tools, and for many it's the first management training they've had.

Before they go, please sit down with each supervisor on the course for 15 minutes, using the guide below. In Broad and Newstrom's research on training transfer, what the learner's manager does before a course ranked as the most powerful point of all.

After the course I'll send you a short follow-up guide for your next catch-up with each of them.

Please reply by Tuesday 2 February to confirm the conversations have happened.

Thanks,
L&D business partner

## Conversation guide (15 minutes)

**1. Why you're going (3 minutes)**

Start with: "I've put you on Lead the Shift because your team's absence is the highest in the region and I've had two complaints about how your one-to-ones go. This is your chance to get on top of it before it goes any further."

**2. What you'll cover (8 minutes)**

Go through the programme content together:

- running a one-to-one
- setting objectives
- giving feedback using SBI
- praise and recognition
- absence conversations and return-to-work meetings
- the absence policy and trigger points
- handling conflict in the team
- planning your week as a new supervisor

Ask which parts they think they'll find hardest.

**3. Support (2 minutes)**

Remind them the course is two days off the job, so they'll need to get ahead on job allocation and timesheet sign-offs the week before and catch up when they're back. Any practice after the course will have to fit around their normal workload.

**4. Follow-up (2 minutes)**

Tell them you'll pick it up at their end-of-year appraisal, where you'll score how well they've applied it as part of their people management rating.
