# Lead the Shift: programme objectives and outline (provider draft v1)

**Programme:** Lead the Shift, first-line management
**For:** 46 engineering supervisors and 18 contact centre team leaders, most promoted from the tools or the phones in the last three years
**Format:** two classroom days in Leeds, four weeks apart, then a 90-minute virtual follow-up in week 8
**Sent by:** the external provider to the Head of L&D, 12 October 2026

## Objectives

By the end of the programme, delegates will:

1. Understand Hallowmere's absence management policy and its trigger points.
2. Be aware of a range of feedback models, including SBI.
3. Appreciate the importance of regular one-to-ones for engagement.
4. Hold effective one-to-ones.
5. Give feedback, coach team members to improve their performance and manage absence in line with policy.
6. Hold a return-to-work conversation with each team member after every absence, using the Hallowmere return-to-work form, before the end of their first shift back.
