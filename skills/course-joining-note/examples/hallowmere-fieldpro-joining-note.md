# FieldPro training: joining note for the Sheffield group (draft for the L&D coordinator to send)

## From the FieldPro project plan

- FieldPro goes live at the Sheffield depot on 1 February 2027. From that date every reactive and planned job is opened, updated and closed in FieldPro, and paper job sheets stop.
- Engineering supervisors will check each engineer's first FieldPro jobs in the week after go-live.
- Engineers have a work phone with FieldPro installed. They don't have work laptops.
- FieldPro has a practice area where people can enter jobs without touching live work.

## Draft note

Subject: FieldPro training, Tuesday 18 January 2027, Sheffield depot

Hi all,

You're booked on FieldPro training.

**When:** Tuesday 18 January 2027, 8:00 to 12:30
**Where:** Sheffield depot, first-floor training room. Park in the yard and sign in at reception.
**Who's going:** engineers and planners based at Sheffield

**What it is:** a morning introduction to FieldPro, the new job-management app, covering job notes, ordering parts, adding photos and customer sign-off.

**Before you come:** please complete the FieldPro e-learning module on the LMS (about 40 minutes). It works best on a laptop, so do it at home in your own time if you can't get on one at the depot.

**Bring:** your work phone, fully charged, and the details of one reactive job you've recently closed on paper, so you can enter it in the practice area.

**Adjustments:** if you have a disability or health condition, let the trainer know when you arrive, and email the L&D coordinator details of your condition so we can keep them on file.

**Food:** tea and coffee provided. The session ends at 12:30, before lunch.

**Questions or running late:** L&D coordinator, 0113 496 0721
