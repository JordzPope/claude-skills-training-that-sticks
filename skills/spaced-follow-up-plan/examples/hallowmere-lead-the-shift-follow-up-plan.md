# Lead the Shift, cohort 3: follow-up plan (draft)

Prepared by: L&D business partner, Operations · Draft for the Head of L&D · Monday 26 October 2026

## The programme

Lead the Shift is Hallowmere's first-line management programme. Cohort 3 is 16 people: 11 engineering supervisors from the Manchester and Sheffield depots and 5 contact centre team leaders from Leeds. Most were promoted from the tools or the phones in the last 12 months. The two-day course runs on Wednesday 4 and Thursday 5 November 2026 at the Leeds office.

What we want them doing afterwards:

- Holding a monthly one-to-one with each person in their team, using the four-part one-to-one structure from day 1.
- Giving feedback on the spot using SBI (situation, behaviour, impact), at least once a week.
- Holding a return-to-work conversation after every absence, using the absence policy and the conversation guide from day 2. Supervisors tell us this comes up two or three times a quarter for most of them.

Engineering supervisors report to the four regional operations managers, who hold a monthly one-to-one with each of them. Team leaders report to the two contact centre managers and meet them weekly. Supervisors spend most of the day on the road between sites and read Teams on their work phones; team leaders are at a desk on the contact centre floor.
