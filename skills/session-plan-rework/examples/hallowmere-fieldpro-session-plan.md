# FieldPro rollout: engineer session plan (draft v2)

From: trainer, Hallowmere Facilities L&D team
Session: half a day at each depot, 12 engineers at a time, one trainer
Kit: projector, printed screen guides, a FieldPro training login on each engineer's work phone
Aim: by the end, engineers can run a reactive job in FieldPro from accepting it to customer sign-off

| Time | Block | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:30 to 08:45 | Welcome | Introductions, why FieldPro is replacing paper job sheets, the go-live date for this depot |
| 08:45 to 09:30 | Tour of the app | Trainer shows every screen on the projector: job list, job detail, notes, parts, photos, timesheet, sign-off |
| 09:30 to 09:50 | Learning styles | Short questionnaire so engineers find out whether they're visual, auditory or kinaesthetic learners. Visual learners get the printed screen guide, auditory learners sit near the front for the walkthrough, kinaesthetic learners follow along on their phones |
| 09:50 to 10:30 | Walkthrough: a job end to end | Engineers first call out, from memory, the screens a job passes through, in order. Then the trainer runs a sample job on the projector, explaining each tap |
| 10:30 to 10:45 | Break | |
| 10:45 to 11:15 | Parts and photos | Trainer demonstrates ordering a part and attaching photos, then plays the supplier's 10-minute video |
| 11:15 to 11:25 | Recall | In pairs, phones face down, engineers write the steps to close a job in the order FieldPro asks for them, then check against the app |
| 11:25 to 11:50 | Hands-on | Engineers use their training login to complete the practice job "Replace kettle, Head Office kitchen" from accepting it to sign-off |
| 11:50 to 12:00 | Close | Any questions, then everyone fills in the feedback form on the LMS |
