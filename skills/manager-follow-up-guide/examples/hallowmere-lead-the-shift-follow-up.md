# Lead the Shift: follow-up sheet for regional operations managers

Drafted by: regional operations manager, North East, for all four regional operations managers to use
For: check-ins with each engineering supervisor after Lead the Shift
Course content: weekly one-to-ones, giving feedback, absence conversations
Course ends: Friday 6 November

Book 15 minutes with each supervisor at the end of your usual weekly call. Let them do most of the talking.

## Day 7 (Friday 13 November)

- You've been doing weekly one-to-ones with your engineers since the course, haven't you?
- Did you use the feedback model from the course?
- What would you like to get better at over the next month?
- What could you try in your next round of one-to-ones?
- What will you do first, and what do you need from me?

If they haven't held a one-to-one yet, remind them this was the main point of the course and note it as an action for their development review.

## Day 30 (week of 7 December)

- Last time you said what you'd try first. How did it go?
- What do you want to focus on this month?
- Tell me about a feedback conversation you've had with one of your engineers since the course. What happened?
- What other ways could you handle a conversation like that next time?
- What will you try next, by when, and how can I help?

When you've seen them handle something well, tell them: "You're a natural with your team now. Keep it up."

## Day 60 (week of 4 January)

- Start by asking how the day 30 action went.
- Looking back over the two months, what's changed in how you run your team?
- What do you want to work on next?
- What options do you have for that?
- What's your next step, and when shall we pick it up?

## After day 60

Score each supervisor 1 to 5 on one-to-ones, feedback and absence conversations. Send the scores, with each supervisor's name, to the people team by 15 January for the half-year performance review.
