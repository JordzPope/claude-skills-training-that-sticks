# Barrier removal plan: FieldPro rollout (draft v1)

Prepared by: L&D business partner, 8 weeks after go-live
Behaviour we want: engineers close every reactive job in FieldPro at the job, before leaving site, with notes, parts used and before-and-after photos.

## What we found

From the FieldPro usage report (weeks 5 to 8) and a short survey answered by 140 engineers and 12 engineering supervisors: about 6 in 10 reactive jobs are closed in the app with notes and photos. The rest are closed on a paper job sheet that a planner types up later. Use of the app is highest at the Newcastle and Manchester depots and lowest at Hull and Sheffield.
