Morning all. One thing to try this week: on your next reactive job, take a before photo in FieldPro before you touch anything, and an after photo before you close the job. It saves a second visit when the client asks what was done. The camera button is at the bottom of the job screen. Thanks, Sam (L&D)

## Message 2

Hi everyone, just a reminder that FieldPro is now live at all four depots and all the training materials are still on the LMS if you need them. Thanks for all your hard work getting used to the new system, it's really appreciated. Sam (L&D)

## Message 3

Hi all,

Job notes are the next big focus for FieldPro and we really need everyone's help to get them right. When a job is reassigned or comes back as a repeat visit, the next engineer relies on your notes to know what's been done, what parts were used, what the client said and what still needs doing. At the moment a lot of notes just say "attended, see previous" or "fixed", which means the next engineer has to start from scratch and the client sees two visits for one fault.

So this week we'd like you to:

1. Complete the 25-minute e-learning module "FieldPro: writing job notes that work" on the LMS (you'll need to log in from a depot PC or a laptop).
2. Do the five-question knowledge check at the end of the module.
3. Use the new job notes structure on every job: what you found, what you did, what's outstanding and what the next engineer needs to know.
4. Look back at the notes on your last three jobs and update any that don't follow the structure.
5. Reply to this message with any feedback on the module so we can improve it for the next group.

If you have any questions at all, your engineering supervisor, the planners and the L&D team are all happy to help, so please don't hesitate to get in touch with any of us. Thanks again for everything you're doing to make FieldPro a success across Hallowmere.

Sam (L&D)
