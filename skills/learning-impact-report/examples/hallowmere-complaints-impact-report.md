# Complaints That Close: impact report for the executive team (draft)

Prepared by: L&D business partner, for the Head of L&D to review before the November executive team meeting
Date: 23 October 2026

## Headline

Complaints That Close has cut reopened complaints by 30% and delivered a return on investment of 540% in its first six months. We recommend running it for every new adviser from January 2027.

## What we did

All 180 customer advisers at the Leeds contact centre attended a one-day Complaints That Close workshop in April and May 2026, in groups of 12. The workshop covered listening, taking ownership of a complaint and getting the right fix first time. Team leaders followed up with each adviser in the six weeks after. The programme cost £41,600, covering the external provider's fees and cover for advisers' time off the phones.

## What changed in behaviour

91% of advisers now confirm the fix with the customer before closing a complaint, and 87% keep ownership of a complaint through to the end without handing it on.

## What results moved

| Measure | January to March 2026 | July to September 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complaints reopened within 28 days | 20% (1 in 5) | 14% |
| Complaints closed at first contact | 48% | 55% |
| Adviser turnover (annualised) | 38% | 35% |

Complaints That Close cut reopened complaints from 1 in 5 to 14%, a 30% reduction, and is behind the rise in complaints closed at first contact. Based on the lower cost of handling reopened complaints, the programme has delivered an ROI of 540%.
