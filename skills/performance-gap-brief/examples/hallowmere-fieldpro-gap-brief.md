# Performance gap brief: FieldPro refresher training (draft v1)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Requested by | Director of Operations |
| For | all 610 engineers (electrical, mechanical and multi-skilled) at the Newcastle, Manchester, Sheffield and Hull depots |
| Prepared by | L&D business partner |
| Date | 20 October 2026 |
| Deadline | before the quarterly contract review with the NHS trust clients in January 2027 |

## The request

"Six months after go-live, engineers still aren't using FieldPro properly. Job notes are thin or missing, there are no photos, and parts aren't logged, so the next engineer turns up without the right part. I want everyone through a FieldPro refresher before the January contract review." (Director of Operations, email, 14 October 2026)

## The result to move

First-time fix rate on reactive jobs: 71% in September 2026 (FieldPro report), against a contract target of 80%.

## The behaviour we need

Engineers need to take ownership of FieldPro and become more digitally confident, so the app is used properly on every job.

## The gap

- Supervisors at all four depots say most engineers can't use the app properly.
- FieldPro report, September 2026: job notes complete on 88% of planned maintenance jobs and 49% of reactive jobs; photos attached on 81% of planned jobs and 37% of reactive jobs; parts logged on 90% of planned jobs and 58% of reactive jobs.
- At go-live in April 2026, 597 of the 610 engineers completed the FieldPro e-learning and passed the depot practical check (logging a test job with notes, parts, photos and customer sign-off).

## What we heard on depot visits

Sheffield and Hull depots, 8 and 9 October 2026: about 20 engineers and four engineering supervisors.

- "On reactive jobs I'm straight on to the next one. The bonus counts jobs closed, so I close it in the van and do the notes later, if I remember."
- "Half the plant rooms and basements have no signal. The photos sit there uploading and then fail, so I give up."
- Supervisors said that when a job needs a revisit, they'll take a phone call from the engineer in place of written notes.

## Causes

1. Engineers lack confidence with FieldPro.
2. Engineers don't see why notes and photos matter to the next engineer.
3. Poor signal in some buildings.
4. The reactive jobs bonus.

## Recommendation

A half-day FieldPro refresher in the classroom at each depot for all 610 engineers, November 2026 to January 2027, with hands-on practice of job notes, parts and photos. The two trainers can run 12 sessions a month between them.

The worst for missing notes is Dave R., a multi-skilled engineer at Hull, so we suggest he goes on the first session and his supervisor checks his notes every week afterwards.

## How we'll know

First-time fix rate on reactive jobs at 80% by 31 March 2027 (FieldPro report).
