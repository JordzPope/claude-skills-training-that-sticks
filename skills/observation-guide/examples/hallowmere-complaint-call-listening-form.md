# Complaints That Close: call listening form

Drafted by: contact centre team leader, for all 18 team leaders to use with their customer advisers
When: two complaint calls per adviser in the month after the course, live (sitting alongside) or from the call recording
Before you listen: tell the adviser which week you'll be listening, give them a copy of this form, and check whether they need any adjustments.

Score each line from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). Add up the scores and divide by the number of lines. 3.5 or above is a pass. Below 3.5, book a second listen and a refresher.

## Overall

Overall impression: is this adviser naturally suited to complaints work, or more of a process person?

## After the listen

Give the adviser their score and two things to work on. Save the completed form in the adviser's folder on the HR drive. Average scores feed into the quarterly performance review, and into probation decisions for advisers in their first six months.
