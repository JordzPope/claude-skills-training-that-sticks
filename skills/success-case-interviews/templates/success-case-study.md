## 5. Interview guides (30 to 40 minutes)

**Success end**

1. Tell me about one specific time you used [skill] at work. When was it?
2. What did you do, step by step?
3. What happened next? What result followed?
4. How could someone else check that result? (a record, a figure, a person by role)
5. What helped you use it: your manager, tools, time, colleagues, something from the course?
6. What do you think would have happened if you hadn't done this?
7. Is there anything else we should know?

**Other end**

1. Tell me about the last time [situation] came up at work. What did you do?
2. Was there a point where you thought about what the course covered? What happened?
3. What made it hard to use? (probe: time, targets, tools, manager, chances to use it, the course itself)
4. What would have needed to be different for you to use it?
5. What, if anything, did you try?
6. Is there anything else we should know?
